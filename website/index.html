Something to know

It helps being British.

In six years at the VA I found it to be something of an advantage. We're not exactly famous for spilling our feelings. If talking openly feels unnatural, that's okay. I'm used to it. You won't get therapist-speak here, just a quiet space to think things through with someone who takes your particular life seriously.
